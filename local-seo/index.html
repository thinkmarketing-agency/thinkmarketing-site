A few straight answers.

Why doesn’t my business show up on Google Maps?

There can be several reasons: eligibility or verification, inaccurate information, relevance to the search, competition or the searcher’s location. Google describes relevance, distance and prominence as key factors. We review the specifics before recommending a fix; nobody can move your business closer to every searcher.

Do I need a website if I already have a Business Profile?

A profile helps people find key details. A website gives you room to explain your services, qualifications, service areas and customer questions. They serve different purposes and should work together.

Can you help with reviews?

Yes. We can scope a reviews display or a process for requesting genuine feedback. We do not write reviews or handle review responses. We don’t create fake reviews, filter requests to seek only positive feedback, or guarantee ratings.

Can you guarantee rankings or leads?

No. We explain the work, the evidence and the limitations. Search results vary by location and over time. Our goal is a clearer, more useful business presence.

Is local SEO included in website hosting?

Profile creation, cleanup and ongoing SEO work are separate. Growth includes a quarterly Search & AI Visibility summary, not a full ongoing SEO service.
