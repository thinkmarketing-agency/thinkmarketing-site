Privacy

Your information. Handled with care.

Last updated November 17, 2026. This notice explains how Think Marketing handles information through this website and direct contact.

When you contact us

If you email, call or text us, we receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, contact details, business information and project questions. We use it to respond, prepare quotes, provide requested services and keep relevant business records. Contact links open your own email, phone or messaging app; this site does not collect messages through a contact form.

Website hosting and technical information

This website is hosted by Vercel. Delivering and protecting the site can involve processing technical information such as your IP address, requested pages, browser information and request times. Vercel may process this information to operate, secure and troubleshoot its services. See Vercel’s Privacy Notice for more information.

Cookies, analytics and AI

We have not added advertising trackers, visitor analytics, marketing cookies or a connected AI chat service. There is no active website assistant or quote builder. Our hosting provider may use technical measures needed for site delivery and security. We will update this notice if we introduce features that change how visitor information is handled.

Service providers and sharing

Hosting, email, phone and messaging providers process information needed to deliver those services. We may also disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect the security of our services. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Retention and security

We retain correspondence and project records as needed to respond to inquiries, deliver services, maintain business records and meet applicable obligations. Retention depends on the purpose and type of record. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system is completely secure. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or sensitive personal information in an inquiry.

Your choices

You can contact us to ask about information you have provided, request a correction or request deletion. We will consider requests and respond as required by applicable law; some records may need to be retained for business or legal obligations. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Links to other websites

Links such as be.cards and the reference links on our service pages take you to other websites. Those sites have their own privacy practices. Information you provide after following a link is handled by that site and its service providers.

Contact Think Marketing

For privacy questions or requests, email thinkmarketingbend@gmail.com or call (541) 316-0735. Think Marketing is based in Bend, Oregon.
