About Think Marketing

Websites and Marketing
Shouldn’t Be Hard.

I’m Jacob,
owner of Think Marketing.

I earned my BBA in Marketing from the University of Georgia (GO DAWGS!). I founded Think Marketing in 2016 with a simple idea. The kind of digital marketing big companies use should be accessible to businesses working with any budget.

I’ve worked with business owners across different industries, with different budgets, goals, and ideas about what growth looks like. I build around what each business needs and agree with the owner on how we’ll measure whether the investment is paying off.

Two questions I ask about every website

  • Is your website an expert on everything you do?
  • Is your website your most valuable employee?

If either answer is no, it might be time for a change.

Websites

A URL is like a plot of land. What do you want to do with it? Build a hotel? Grow crops? Construct a highway? What you build depends on what you need it to do.

I think about websites the same way. Maybe you want a place to explain your services and take bookings. Maybe you have a bigger idea and aren’t sure what it would take to build. I want to hear the whole idea. At Think Marketing, I help you turn it into a plan that fits your budget, with a clear sense of what to build first and what can come later.

Digital marketing

One of the biggest complaints I hear from owners is, “I’m paying a lot, and I don’t know what I’m getting.” That’s a fair complaint. Digital marketing is just as customizable as a website, though some agencies prefer to sell it in fixed packages.

You can build a strategy around $100 a month, $100,000 a month, or anything in between. What changes is what you can reasonably expect that budget to accomplish. I start with a website that works for you behind the scenes and gives search engines a clear path to find your pages. Then I look at what your customers are searching for, how and where you can serve them, and what you want them to do next.

The tools we have today make it easier to show where the money went and what happened as a result. You should know what went to ads, what work I did, and what we can measure in calls, bookings, or inquiries. When the numbers point to a change, we talk through it and adjust.

AI

AI makes it easier to build a website yourself. In experienced hands, it can also help you get more out of the site you build. I use it in my work at Think Marketing, and Jasper, my AI assistant, is one example. You can give him a website to review, ask what a similar site might cost, or have him send me a summary when you’re ready to talk.

I build websites so customers, search engines, and AI tools can understand what a business does. That starts with clear service pages, useful answers, and a site that’s easy to navigate and crawl. The industry is still learning how to respond to and work with AI (don’t let anyone tell you differently). I look at visits from AI tools, crawler activity, and whether AI answers cite the business. Those signals help me decide what to check or improve next.
