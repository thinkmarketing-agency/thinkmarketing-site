About Think Marketing

Your domain is the land.
Let’s build what your business needs.

A domain name is a little like a plot of land. It gives you a place to build, but what you do with it depends on your vision.

From a starting point to something useful

You could build a house, a hotel, or a working farm. Each serves a different purpose and needs a different plan.

Your website is much the same. It might introduce your business, answer customer questions, book appointments, collect inquiries, or support daily operations.

Think Marketing is the bridge between that starting point and what you want to create.

Custom websites for small and medium-sized businesses

Based in Bend, Oregon, Think Marketing helps businesses turn their ideas into useful websites.

We start by understanding what you do, who your customers are, and what you want your website to accomplish. That understanding shapes the design, information, and functionality we recommend.

Two questions guide the work.

Question one

Is your website an expert on what you do?

Your website should explain your services clearly and answer the questions customers ask before contacting you. Your experience and expertise should come through.

We organize the information you provide into a website customers can understand.

Question two

Is your website your most valuable employee?

That’s the goal we work toward. A useful website can answer questions after hours, guide an inquiry, help someone book an appointment, or make a routine task easier.

We help you identify where your website can contribute and what makes sense to build.

A clear plan and a practical investment

You should understand what you’re buying. We explain the scope, upfront cost, monthly service, and responsibilities before work begins.

Your website can grow with your business. Our job is to help you build with purpose at each stage.
