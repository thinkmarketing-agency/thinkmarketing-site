About Think Marketing

Good websites start
with good questions.

I’m Jacob. I build custom websites and help small and medium-sized businesses make practical marketing decisions. Think Marketing is based in Bend, Oregon.

Your domain is the land.

A domain gives you a place to build. What belongs there depends on your vision: a home for your expertise, a place to book appointments, or a tool that makes daily work easier.

Think Marketing helps bridge that starting point and what you want to create.

Two questions guide my work.

Is your website an expert on what you do?

It should explain your services and answer the questions your customers ask.

Is your website your most valuable employee?

That’s the goal: a website that helps customers and takes useful work off your plate.

Let’s start with your business.

Tell me what you do, who you serve, and what you want to improve. We’ll work out a sensible next step together.
