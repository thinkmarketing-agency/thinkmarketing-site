A few straight answers.

Is AI visibility different from SEO?

There is substantial overlap: discoverable pages, clear information and useful content matter to both. Google says its AI search features do not require special optimization beyond established SEO fundamentals. We focus on practical improvements rather than a secret formula.

What are AEO and GEO?

Answer Engine Optimization and Generative Engine Optimization are names used for work intended to improve visibility in AI-generated answers. We use plain English: make business information easier to find, understand and reference, then observe what happens.

What does the $350 review deliver?

A review of up to 10 pages, discovery/technical/content checks and 5 customer questions sampled across 2 agreed AI services. Your report records dates, sources, observed gaps, limitations and prioritized recommendations. It is an assessment; fixes and new content are quoted separately.

What is included in Growth’s quarterly summary?

A brief review of available search performance and identifiable AI referral data, plus a small agreed set of dated visibility observations and recommended next steps. Data availability, questions and access are confirmed in your proposal. This does not include a full audit, monthly SEO management or new content production.

Can you guarantee that ChatGPT or Google will recommend me?

No. Answers vary by service, wording, location and time. Allowing a search crawler to access your pages does not guarantee inclusion. We distinguish measured observations from recommendations and avoid universal AI visibility scores.

Does this put a chatbot on my website?

No. This service concerns how your business is found in search and AI answers. Website assistants are a separate service.
