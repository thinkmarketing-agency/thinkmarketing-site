FAQ

A few straight answers.

The practical details, when you need them.

What does a website cost?

Essential is $1,995 plus $50/month. Growth is $2,500 plus $80/month. Enterprise is $400/month for 12 months ($4,800 total). Compare the packages. Extra capabilities are included in your proposal before you commit.

What does the monthly fee cover?

Essential and Growth include hosting, basic monitoring, and one or two minor updates per month respectively. Additional systems include their agreed standard tools and support within defined limits. Advertising spend, payment processing, taxes, and exceptional purchases are separate when applicable.

How do payments and ownership work?

Essential and Growth development is paid 50% to start and 50% on completion. You own the site after development is paid, with month-to-month hosting. Enterprise is a 12-month commitment starting at kickoff; you own the site after all 12 payments. Hosting and support after year one are quoted separately.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes, with an agreed editing system. We define what staff need to change and show you how. The standard packages include small updates performed by Think Marketing; staff editing is a separately scoped capability.

What do you need from me?

Your business information, final written content, images, and branding. We organize those materials into the website. Content production and review responses are outside our services. We confirm the schedule once the scope and materials are clear.

What if I need more later?

We discuss the work and any price change before starting. A minor update is a small change to existing hours, contact details, or a short text correction. Extra work billed by time is $75/hour, with a $37.50 minimum. Existing agreements remain in effect.

Can you improve my existing website?

We can assess it first. A targeted improvement may be a better investment than a rebuild. We explain what your platform supports and what makes sense for your goals.

Do I need local SEO, AI visibility, or paid ads?

That depends on how your customers look for help. We start with their intent and your service area, then recommend the work that fits. Local SEO and AI/search visibility often overlap; shared work is scoped once. See our approach to marketing.

Can you guarantee rankings or leads?

No. We agree on the work, track useful measures where data is available, and explain what the results show. Search rankings, AI recommendations, and lead volumes cannot be guaranteed.

Can I move my website elsewhere?

We help coordinate a reasonable transfer of your paid-for website. Your domain should be registered in your name or business name. Provider licensing limits and additional transfer work are explained before proceeding. Enterprise early departure terms are defined in the proposal.
