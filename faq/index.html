FAQ

A few straight answers.

The practical details, when you need them.

What does a website cost?

Essential is $1,995 to build plus $50/month, with additional capabilities scoped as needed. Enterprise covers larger websites and is individually priced; monthly development plans are available. See the options and example configurations. Your complete quote shows setup, monthly service, any commitment, and what is included.

What does the monthly fee cover?

Essential includes hosting, basic monitoring, and one minor update per month. Additional systems include their agreed standard tools and support within defined limits. Enterprise support and hosting are defined in its proposal. Advertising spend, payment processing, taxes, and exceptional purchases are separate when applicable.

How do payments and ownership work?

Essential development is paid 50% to start and 50% on completion. You own the site after development is paid, with month-to-month hosting. Enterprise payment schedules, any minimum commitment, ownership, and post-term hosting/support are agreed in the proposal before work starts. Existing client agreements remain in effect.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes, with an agreed editing system. We define what staff need to change and show you how. The standard packages include small updates performed by Think Marketing; staff editing is a separately scoped capability.

What do you need from me?

Your business information, final written content, images, and branding. We organize those materials into the website. Content production and review responses are outside our services. We confirm the schedule once the scope and materials are clear.

What if I need more later?

We discuss the work and any price change before starting. A minor update is a small change to existing hours, contact details, or a short text correction. Additional work is quoted before starting. Existing agreements remain in effect.

Can you improve my existing website?

We can assess it first. A targeted improvement may be a better investment than a rebuild. We explain what your platform supports and what makes sense for your goals.

Do I need local SEO, AI visibility, or paid ads?

That depends on how your customers look for help. We start with their intent and your service area, then recommend the work that fits. Local SEO and AI/search visibility often overlap; shared work is scoped once. See our approach to marketing.

Can you guarantee rankings or leads?

No. We agree on the work, track useful measures where data is available, and explain what the results show. Search rankings, AI recommendations, and lead volumes cannot be guaranteed.

Can I move my website elsewhere?

We help coordinate a reasonable transfer of your paid-for website. Your domain should be registered in your name or business name. Provider licensing limits and additional transfer work are explained before proceeding. Enterprise early departure terms are defined in the proposal.

I’m already paying for digital marketing, but I don’t understand what I’m getting. Can you help?

Yes. We can review the reports and information you have, explain the terminology, and help you understand what the numbers show—and what they leave unanswered. We can also help you prepare questions for your current provider and identify information needed to evaluate the work. Verifying campaign activity or results may require account access and a separately agreed review. You don’t need to switch providers or buy an ongoing service to get advice.

What should I bring to a consultation?

Your questions, recent reports, and a description of what you’re paying for. An existing proposal or service agreement can help us understand the promised deliverables. Please don’t send passwords; we’ll arrange appropriate access if needed. A 60-minute consultation is $125. Additional investigation or implementation is agreed separately.
