Need your website to do more?

Staff editing, appointment scheduling, business email, reviews displays, payments, additional domains, and connected workflows can be included in a configuration built around your needs.

What your monthly payment covers

Essential and Growth include hosting, basic monitoring, and the listed update allowance. Additional configurations include the agreed software, infrastructure, and support for those systems. Your proposal identifies features and usage limits.

A few things to know

Content: you supply final written content, images, branding, and business information. Content production is outside these packages.

Minor updates: small changes to existing hours, contact details, or a short text correction. New pages and features are separately scoped.

Additional work: $75/hour, $37.50 minimum, agreed before starting. Existing client agreements remain in effect.

Ownership: your website is yours after development is paid. Third-party software retains its provider’s terms. We help coordinate a reasonable transfer if you leave.

Larger projects and online stores

Enterprise, e-commerce, and specialized projects receive an individual proposal. Enterprise plans start at $400/month with a 12-month commitment, billed from kickoff—at least $4,800 over the initial term. Scope, support, ownership, and any early departure obligation are defined in the proposal.

For online stores, standard platform costs are included in the agreed monthly configuration. Payment processing and transaction fees remain separate.
