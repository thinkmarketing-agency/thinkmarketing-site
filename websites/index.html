Know the whole price.

Essential development is paid 50% to start and 50% on completion. Its hosting is month-to-month after development is paid. You provide final written content, images, and branding.

Your proposal sets out the build/setup cost, monthly service, any minimum commitment, ownership, and what happens after the initial term. Enterprise is individually scoped; it has no universal monthly price.
