Business facts & approach

Think Marketing · Bend, Oregon · Custom websites and digital marketing for small and medium-sized businesses in Central Oregon. Contact Jacob: thinkmarketingbend@gmail.com or (541) 316-0735.

A domain is like a plot of land; Think Marketing helps connect the starting point and the business’s vision. Two design goals: a website that is an expert on what the business does, and a website that works like a valuable employee. These are goals, not performance guarantees.

Digital marketing starts with customer intent, geography and useful next steps. Services include consulting, explanation of marketing reports, Local SEO, AI discoverability assessment, and Google Search PPC. No general content production or review responses. PPC campaign ad text is included in PPC.

Monthly service fees include standard software and infrastructure within the listed service limits. No separate standard tool fee.

Scope Limit

Before exceeding limits, agree a new bundled tier. Never invent overage rates or silently add charges. Where a hard cap is possible, stop at the cap; monitoring and client communication are still required.

Exceptions

Advertising spend; Payment processing/transaction fees; Taxes; Premium or high-renewal domains outside the standard allowance; Optional paid directory placements

Estimate

Informational estimate, valid 30 days, subject to requirements, availability and owner confirmation. From items produce a starting estimate, never a fixed total.

Discounts

Only owner-approved discounts with explicit SKU, amount and duration. Jasper cannot independently waive setup or change monthly prices.

Setup

Installation products have listed setup fees unless explicitly included in another product. Onboarding-only fees apply only when listed. Do not invent setup charges for advisory sessions or already included functionality.

Cancellation

Proposed add-ons are month-to-month, with cancellation before next renewal. Annual third-party commitments are internal costs unless explicitly agreed otherwise. Do not change an existing customer agreement.

Billing

Essential development: 50% to start and 50% on completion. Add-on charges begin at activation. Enterprise billing, minimum term, ownership and continued support are defined in its proposal. Existing agreements retain their terms.

Included Support

Support allowances are per calendar month and do not roll over. They cover the installed configuration; new features, content production and major revisions are excluded. Additional work is quoted and agreed before starting. Existing agreements remain in effect.

Hosting

Feature add-ons do not include the base website hosting unless expressly stated. Determine whether the customer already has suitable hosting. Do not force a new website purchase for standalone email, scheduling or domains.

Exclusions

No general copywriting, photography/video production, social content creation or review responses. Campaign ad text is included only in PPC.

Custom Work

Custom project prices are starting estimates until requirements and delivery are confirmed.

Assistant Status

No live Jasper chat or customer-facing estimate builder is active. This reference can be explicitly loaded by a future assistant. Presence of this page does not connect or train an AI.

Website Options

New projects use Essential as the defined starting website, plus scoped additions, or Enterprise for an individually scoped larger project. Growth is legacy only. Enterprise requires a custom quote; do not invent a starting price.

Readiness

This is a proposed comprehensive menu. Existing fixed entries still require scope confirmation; new or unbuilt capabilities require implementation and supplier-cost validation before a final offer. A starting price is not proof that a system is already built.

Internal Guidance

Off-menu work requires a requirements brief and owner-reviewed estimate. Internal effort guidance is supplied separately to a future server-side assistant and is not part of this public-readable reference.

Overlap

Ask which site/location/workflow is shared. Included child capabilities are not separately charged for the same work. Missing prerequisites, excess usage, or ambiguous overlaps must be resolved before presenting a complete estimate.

Quote Presentation

Customer estimates show build/setup, monthly service, applicable commitments, inclusions, exclusions and assumptions. No itemized internal time rate or internal minimum is displayed.

Portfolio

Future portfolio examples must describe what a comparable configuration would cost today, dated and scoped; never expose client invoices. Price each example from current catalog IDs and quantities. Jasper may start from these examples once they exist, but no examples are currently connected.

Needs First

Lead with the smallest complete solution. Keep optional services separate. Never automatically bundle local SEO or monitoring because a customer asks to be found. Assess existing email and aliases. Discuss paid search for time-bound demand after clarifying intent, geography, capacity, budget and measurement; it does not replace organic search setup.

Seo Deliverables

Extra SEO must identify paid work: existing-site assessment, profile creation, listing corrections, or agreed technical/information-structure fixes. Never resell Essential search foundation on the same build. Ongoing SEO requires an assessed recurring backlog and explicit deliverables; do not default to the $395 service. Do not stack included profile maintenance or monitoring.

How to prepare an estimate

  1. Choose the starting context: Essential, existing suitable website, standalone business tool, or Enterprise. Enterprise and bespoke systems require individual scoping.
  2. Map the visitor’s request to a capability and ask the clarification question. Do not assume that “calendar” means paid booking or that “portal” means a simple editable page.
  3. Use only listed numbers within the stated scope. First-seat setup differs from additional-seat setup where specified. Included functionality is not an extra.
  4. Resolve prerequisites and overlaps. Never add fees for the same workflow twice. A report-review discussion, for example, is not a second consultation.
  5. For off-menu work, gather systems, users, volume, data, requested behavior, timing and access constraints. A future server-side assistant receives separate private owner guidance; no internal rates are in this reference.
  6. Show setup/build, recurring price, any commitment, scope, tool inclusion, applicable exceptions, assumptions and unresolved questions. From prices remain starting estimates. Missing supplier costs or unknown effort require referral, not invention.
  7. Owner confirmation is required for final scope, readiness, timing, custom prices and discounts. No existing client agreement is changed by this menu.

Worked example

Three inboxes: $200 setup + $30/month. Three public booking seats: $400 setup + $105/month. One basic admin: $750 setup + $65/month. Two eligible additional domains: $150 setup + $10/month. Total $1,500 setup + $210/month, excluding existing hosting and taxes. With Essential: $3,495 setup + $260/month. Remove scheduling if ordinary email calendars are all that is needed.
